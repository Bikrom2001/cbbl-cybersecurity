Prerequisites

Experience required, per track

Applicants submit a short CV and a statement of experience. A 15-minute technical conversation confirms fit.

2+ years security or systems
Knowledge: Networking Linux Web

Red Team Operations

3+ years, prior testing
Knowledge: Active Directory Scripting

Blue Team & SOC

1+ year SOC or IT ops
Knowledge: Log sources SIEM Networking

Digital Forensics

2+ years IT or security
Knowledge: File systems OS internals

2+ years cloud or infra
Knowledge: IAM Networking Containers

Cybersecurity GRC

2+ years security, audit or risk
Knowledge: Control frameworks Policy

Assessment

One deliverable, four criteria

40%

Technical accuracy

25%

Depth of analysis

20%

Quality of written deliverable

15%

Communication in debrief

Pass requires 70% overall and no criterion below 50%. Feedback is written and specific.

Lab rules

All exercises run inside CBBL's isolated lab. Techniques learned are not applied to systems without written authorisation. Participants sign an acceptable-use agreement.

Offensive tracks teach attack techniques in order to defend against them — within a controlled environment and a professional ethics framework.
