About us

A Bangladeshi technology company built around capability

Creating Better Bangladesh Limited was established in 2016 to do two connected things: help organisations use technology securely, and help people build skills that employers actually need.

Who we are

CBBL is an independent Bangladeshi company. Our trade licence covers information technology services, software development, IT importing, e-commerce, export and general supply (excluding chemicals).

We work across three connected areas: security and infrastructure, software delivery, and training with career support. Because the same organisation does all three, our training reflects what we see in live environments — and our consulting benefits from years of explaining hard concepts to people who are new to them.

How we work

Define the scope

What will be done, by whom, when, and what it costs — written before work starts.

Name the deliverable

A report, a running system, a documented curriculum, an assessment record.

State the limits

If something is outside our capability, we say so and coordinate a specialist.

Protect confidentiality

Client work is confidential; public case studies are anonymised.
