Trust

What we are, what we are not, and how to check

Most organisations overstate credentials. This page exists so that every claim on this site can be traced. Where something is pending or absent, it says so plainly rather than implying it.

Why this page exists

A buyer's first question about a security firm is a fair one: can you prove what you claim? Below is the current position on every credential, relationship and accreditation that appears anywhere on this site. If a statement here conflicts with another page, this page takes precedence.

Corporate relationship

CBBL is an independent Bangladeshi limited company, established in 2016, and a sister concern of the PeopleNTech group.

PeopleNTech Institute of IT is the group's training division. CBBL and PeopleNTech operate from the same Dhaka address and share group functions such as facilities and quality assurance, while remaining separate legal entities with their own registrations and accounts.

Where PeopleNTech appears on this site — a group logo strip, a testimonial from a group colleague, a shared facility — it is presented as a group relationship, not as a client endorsement or a customer reference. We do not present a sister company as a satisfied client.

Verification: Company registration number, trade licence number and TIN are supplied on request or in a tender submission. They are not published on this page because published identifiers are frequently misused in invoice fraud.

How we describe courses

There is a real difference between teaching to a certification blueprint and being authorised to deliver a vendor's official curriculum. We use the first phrasing, deliberately:

  • “Teaches to the CEH blueprint as exam preparation” — accurate today.
  • “Authorised EC-Council training centre” — not accurate, and never used.
  • “CBBL Certificate of Completion” — issued by us, for our own assessment.
  • “Vendor certification” — issued by the vendor after their exam, entirely separate from us.

Security and data handling

Client data

Minimised at collection, masked where possible, held only for the engagement, and deleted on an agreed schedule. Findings belong to the client.

Training material

Client findings are never used as teaching material without written consent; where used, they are anonymised and the client approves the wording.

Authorisation

No system is tested without a signed scope naming the target, the window and the authorised signatory. This applies to labs as well as live work.

Incident contact

A named security contact is provided in every engagement letter, with an out-of-hours route for active incidents.

How to verify a claim

If any statement on this site matters to your decision, ask for the evidence. We will provide, under NDA where appropriate: registration and tax documents, instructor CVs and certifications, sample reports with client data removed, and references from clients who have agreed to be contacted.

If we cannot evidence a claim, we will withdraw it rather than defend it.
