Case study

Assessment for a mid-sized financial institution

The problem

The client had completed an external audit with findings they did not fully understand, and no agreed sequence for fixing them. Two internal teams disagreed about priority.

Approach

  1. Scoping session with IT, risk and operations to agree what “important” meant for them.
  2. Technical review of the internal network, identity configuration and the customer-facing application.
  3. Validation of each finding, with evidence and a realistic exploitation path where one existed.
  4. Remediation workshop to agree owners and sequence.

Outcome

A single prioritised roadmap, agreed by both teams, with 14 of 22 findings closed within one quarter. The remaining items were accepted with documented compensating controls.

Case study

Closed-cohort training for a technology team

The problem

A product team of nine was shipping quickly with no consistent security practice, and had failed an internal review. Generic courses had not changed behaviour.

Approach

  1. Skills baseline and interviews with three team leads.
  2. Curriculum built around their own codebase and deployment pipeline.
  3. Eight weekly sessions, each ending in a task applied to a real repository.
  4. Final assessment: a threat model and a code review of a peer's work.

Outcome

All nine participants passed. Security review findings on the next release dropped substantially, and two internal champions now run the practice monthly.
