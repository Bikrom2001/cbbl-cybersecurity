Exam vouchers, with the terms explained before you pay

We help learners and teams obtain certification exam vouchers and plan the exam properly. This page states exactly what we are, what we are not, and what happens if an exam does not go to plan.

Status disclosure CBBL is not currently an authorised reseller or official partner of any certification vendor. Vouchers are procured on your behalf through authorised channels, and the vendor's own terms govern the exam. We do not claim partner status, discount agreements or guaranteed pricing. Status verified: [date to be inserted at publication].

How it works

Four steps, no surprises

Confirm the exam

Vendor, exam code, version and current fee — checked against the vendor's own page.

Written quotation

Voucher cost, our handling fee and the validity period, itemised.

Payment and issue

Voucher code issued after payment clears, with an activation guide.

Exam booking

You book with the vendor's testing provider. We do not book on your behalf.
