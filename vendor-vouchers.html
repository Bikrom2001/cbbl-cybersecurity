Exam vouchers, with the terms explained before you pay

We help learners and teams obtain certification exam vouchers and plan the exam properly. This page states exactly what we are, what we are not, and what happens if an exam does not go to plan.

Home > Training > Vendor Exam Vouchers

How it works

Four steps, no surprises

01

Confirm the exam

Vendor, exam code, version and current fee — checked against the vendor's own page.

02

Written quotation

Voucher cost, our handling fee and the validity period, itemised.

03

Payment and issue

Voucher code issued after payment clears, with an activation guide.

04

Exam booking

You book with the vendor's testing provider. We do not book on your behalf.

What is included

Be clear about the boundary

Included
  • Exam voucher procurement
  • Written quotation with validity period
  • Activation guidance
  • Exam-readiness guidance
  • Receipt for reimbursement
Not included
  • Exam scheduling with the testing provider
  • Guaranteed pass or score improvement
  • Retake vouchers (unless the vendor includes them)
  • Vendor membership or continuing-education fees
  • Vendor policy decisions or appeals
Voucher validity, transferability and refund rules are set by the vendor. We pass them on in writing before payment.
