TRAINING · INSTITUTIONAL

Professional training for institutional and public-safety teams

CBBL delivers structured technology and cybersecurity training to institutional clients by arrangement. Enquiries are handled confidentially by a designated coordinator, and no client is named publicly without written permission.

Subject areas

Eight areas we can deliver

Each area is taught at awareness, practitioner or advanced level, depending on the audience and the outcome agreed in the training needs analysis.

Digital evidence handling

Principles of preservation, documentation and chain of custody for digital material.

Cybercrime awareness

Threat landscape, common fraud patterns and reporting pathways for non-technical staff.

Network investigation fundamentals

Reading network logs and understanding traffic patterns in an investigative context.

Mobile device security

Device hygiene, data protection and secure communication practices.

Incident coordination

Roles, escalation and inter-unit communication during a technology incident.

Secure communications

Practical guidance on protecting sensitive communication channels.

Open-source intelligence discipline

Lawful collection, source evaluation, record keeping and ethical boundaries.

Train-the-trainer

Preparing internal instructors to deliver and assess the same curriculum consistently.

Engagement

Five stages, all documented

01

Confidential enquiry

Via the form below or a direct call. A designated coordinator responds.

02

NDA and authorisation

Mutual NDA signed; a named authorised representative confirmed.

03

Needs analysis

Audience profile, current capability and the outcome required.

04

Delivery

On-site or at a CBBL facility, with attendance and assessment records.

Confidentiality

How we protect institutional work

01

No client name, logo or photograph is published without written permission.

02

Participant records are held separately and are not used in marketing.

03

Training materials are versioned and issued to the authorised representative only.

04

Instructors sign a confidentiality undertaking before delivery.

05

Any request outside our competence is declined in writing rather than improvised.

CBBL does not claim government endorsement, authorisation or a formal partnership unless it has been granted and can be evidenced in writing.
