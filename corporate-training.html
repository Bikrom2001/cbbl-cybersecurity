Training · Corporate

Training designed around your risk, not our catalogue

We start with a needs assessment, agree measurable outcomes, then build the curriculum. Every programme ends with an assessment report you can take to management.

Request a Training Proposal How needs assessment works

What we deliver

Nine components, assembled to fit

Custom curriculum design

Built from your systems, roles and risk profile — not a generic slide deck.

Needs assessment

Interviews, a skills matrix and a short practical baseline. Written gap analysis first.

Executive awareness

A 90-minute session on risk in business terms, decision rights and incident governance.

Employee awareness

Role-based modules for staff, finance, HR and IT, plus a simulated phishing exercise with agreed rules.

Technical upskilling

Hands-on training for IT and security teams on network defence, cloud, secure development and IR.

Team-based labs

Scenario exercises where the team works together on a realistic incident, assessment or design task.

Delivery options

On-site, live online, hybrid, or a closed cohort at a CBBL lab. Shift patterns can be accommodated.

Post-training assessment

Practical assessment against agreed outcomes, plus a written report and skills matrix.

Corporate certificate

A CBBL Certificate of Completion naming the programme and the assessment result.

Process

Seven steps, three weeks to a proposal

Enquiry

30-minute call to understand the request.

Needs assessment

Gap analysis and outcome statement, week 1–2.

Proposal

Curriculum, schedule, logistics and fee, week 2–3.

Delivery & assessment

Delivery, then a practical assessment and report.
