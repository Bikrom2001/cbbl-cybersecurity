How to choose

Answer three questions

1. Where are you now?

No IT background → Computer Operation or IT Support. Working in IT → networking, Linux or cloud. In security already → advanced tracks.

2. What do you need it for?

A first job → professional training plus placement support. A specialisation → advanced track. A team gap → corporate training.

3. How much time do you have?

6–8 hours a week → evening course. Full-time → intensive track or NSDA programme.
