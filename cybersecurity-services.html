What we help with

Twelve security services, one method

Each service below is described the same way: what it is, who it is for, what we do and what you receive. No proprietary-product claims.

Cybersecurity consultancy

Structured advice on reducing technology risk against your business priorities. You receive a prioritised 6–12 month roadmap.

Request a scoping call
Most requested

Security assessment and vulnerability assessment support

Independent review of your environment, findings validated and ranked by real exploitability and business impact.

Penetration testing coordination

Qualified testers, CBBL-managed scope, scheduling, evidence handling and remediation follow-up. Written authorisation required.

Security awareness training

Role-specific sessions that change behaviour: phishing, social engineering, data handling and reporting.

Network security consulting

Segmentation, firewall policy, remote access and monitoring design, with a migration plan.

Cloud security consulting

Identity, data protection and logging review for cloud environments, with prioritised configuration changes.

Endpoint and infrastructure guidance

Hardening baselines for servers and endpoints, with a rollout checklist your team can follow.

Incident-response readiness

Plan, severity matrix, roles, escalation paths and a tabletop exercise with a written improvement plan.

Policy and compliance advisory

Policy set review, drafting and an obligations-to-controls mapping. Advisory only — we do not certify compliance.

IT audit support

Technical evidence gathering, control review and observation logs for internal or external auditors.

Managed security coordination

Monitoring delivered by a specialist partner, with CBBL managing scope, onboarding and service reviews. We do not claim an in-house 24/7 SOC.

Solution implementation support

Requirements, vendor evaluation, deployment, tuning and runbooks for third-party security technology.

IT solutions

Infrastructure that someone owns

Design, supply, deploy and support — with a named owner for each element and documentation your team can actually use.

Network design and deployment

Switching, routing, wireless and segmentation, delivered with an as-built diagram and an IP register.

02

Server and storage

Physical and virtual server platforms, backup design, and a restore test that proves the backup works.

Requirements, vendor comparison, import and delivery — with warranty and support terms stated in writing.

Cloud and hybrid migration

How an engagement runs

Five steps, each with an output

01

Scoping call

30–45 minutes, no cost. Problem, environment, constraint.

02

Written scope

Objectives, targets, exclusions, schedule, roles and fee.

03

Delivery

Work within the agreed window with a named lead.

04

Reporting

Validated findings written for technical and management readers.

What you receive

A report your whole team can act on

Executive summary

The risk in business terms with a prioritised top five.

Findings

Description, evidence reference, severity, business impact, specific remediation.

Remediation plan

Sequenced by risk and effort, with an owner column.

Appendix

Scope, method summary, limitations and glossary.

We do not deliver raw scanner output as a report.

Scope, limits and confidentiality

  • Signed scope and NDA before work.
  • Written authorisation required to test any system.
  • Client data minimised, masked and deleted.
  • Findings belong to the client.
  • Partner-delivered work is named as such.
