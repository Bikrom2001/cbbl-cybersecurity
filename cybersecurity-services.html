How an engagement runs

Five steps, each with an output

Scoping call

30–45 minutes, no cost. Problem, environment, constraint. Output: draft scope.

Written scope

Objectives, targets, exclusions, schedule, roles and fee. Output: signed scope.

Delivery

Work within the agreed window with a named lead. Output: evidence and interim findings.

Reporting

Validated findings written for technical and management readers. Output: report and plan.

What you receive

A report three people can use

  • Executive summary — the risk in business terms with a prioritised top five.
  • Findings — description, evidence reference, severity, business impact, specific remediation.
  • Remediation plan — sequenced by risk and effort, with an owner column.
  • Appendix — scope, method summary, limitations and glossary.

We do not deliver raw scanner output as a report.

Scope, limits and confidentiality

  • Signed scope and NDA before work.
  • Written authorisation required to test any system.
  • Client data minimised, masked and deleted.
  • Findings belong to the client.
  • Partner-delivered work is named as such.
