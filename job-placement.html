Placement support

A structured process, honest feedback, real introductions

Placement support is included with every CBBL course. It is a process, not a promise: we prepare you properly, introduce you where your profile fits, and tell you the truth after every interview.

Talk to the placement team Hire a CBBL graduate
No job guarantee

CBBL does not guarantee employment, a salary level or a placement timeline, and we do not advertise "100% job placement". Any organisation that does should be treated with caution. What we commit to is the process below, delivered by a named counsellor.

What you get

Eleven kinds of support

Career counselling

A one-to-one session mapping your background to realistic entry roles.

CV development

A CV rewritten around evidence of skill, not a list of courses attended.

LinkedIn optimisation

Profile, headline and a simple content plan you can sustain.

Portfolio building

Documented lab work and projects you can show and explain.

Mock interviews

Technical and HR practice with written feedback after each round.

Technical interview drills

Scenario questions drawn from real entry-level interviews.

Employer introductions

Where your profile matches an opening and the employer agrees.

Job-search strategy

Where the roles are, how to apply, and how to follow up without pestering.

Salary guidance

Realistic ranges by role and experience, with the source of the figure.

Process

Five stages after your last class

Stage 1

Profile review

Within 10 working days of course completion.

Stage 2

Readiness plan

Written actions with dates: CV, portfolio, practice.

Stage 3

Preparation

Mock interviews and drills until you are ready.

Stage 4

Introductions

Only where the match is genuine.

Stage 5

Debrief & Iteration

Feedback from interview rounds to hone subsequent submissions.

What “support” means in practice

Activity Who does it Timing
Career counselling session Placement counsellor Within 10 working days of completion
CV and LinkedIn review Counsellor, written feedback Within 5 working days of submission
Mock interview Counsellor or technical instructor On request, up to 3 rounds
Employer introduction Counsellor When a matching opening exists
Post-interview feedback Counsellor Within 3 working days
