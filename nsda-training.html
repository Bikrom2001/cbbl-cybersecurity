Assessment

What "competent" means here

01
Formative

Lab tasks reviewed by the instructor after each module.

02
Summative

A practical task against an observation checklist, plus a knowledge component where required.

03
Evidence record

The assessor documents what was observed against each criterion.

04
Result

Competent or not yet competent — with a written gap list and one re-assessment opportunity.

05
Moderation

A second assessor reviews a sample for consistency.

Certification

On successful assessment you receive a CBBL Statement of Competency listing the units achieved and the assessment date, and a CBBL Certificate of Completion.

We do not state or imply that a CBBL certificate is an NSDA certificate unless that is formally granted and documented.
