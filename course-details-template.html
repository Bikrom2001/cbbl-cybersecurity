Ethical Hacking Fundamentals

Beginner Professional Hybrid

A ten-week, lab-based introduction to offensive security practice: how systems are attacked, how to prove it safely, and how to write a report a client can act on.

Duration 10 weeks · 2 evenings
Effort 6–8 hours per week
Mode Hybrid (Online + Labs)

What you will be able to do

By the end of the course, with the lab environment and your notes, you can:

  • Plan a scoped assessment and explain the legal boundary in writing.
  • Perform structured reconnaissance on an authorised target.
  • Identify and validate common web and network weaknesses.
  • Explain the business impact of a finding in plain language.
  • Write a findings report with evidence, severity and remediation.
  • Present findings in a short client-style debrief.

Curriculum

Authorisation, scope documents, rules of engagement, and the difference between testing and unauthorised access. Lab setup and safety rules.
Passive and active discovery, DNS and certificate sources, service enumeration, and recording evidence as you go.
Authentication and session issues, injection classes, access-control failures and file handling — validated manually, not just flagged by a scanner.
Service misconfiguration, credential hygiene, lateral-movement concepts and the controls that would have prevented each.
Reproducing a finding, capturing minimal evidence, avoiding data exposure, and deciding when a finding is real.
Severity that reflects business impact, remediation that a developer can implement, and a ten-minute debrief you can deliver calmly.
